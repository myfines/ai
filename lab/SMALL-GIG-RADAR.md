# Small Gig Radar

Goal: find small, fast, zero-upfront-cost paid tasks suitable for an AI-assisted operator.

## Hard rules

- We receive money; we do not pay deposits, activation fees, recharge balances, guarantees, or "unlock" fees.
- Prefer escrowed/funded bounties with explicit acceptance criteria.
- Never count a claim as revenue until accepted and paid.
- Avoid spam, fake identities, fabricated proof, unauthorized automation, and work that requires misleading users.
- Prefer tasks that can be completed and evidenced in hours rather than long hiring processes.

Preferred scalable loop:

2. verify funding + acceptance criteria + competition;
3. claim/contact;
4. execute with AI;
5. publish sanitized proof;
6. submit;
7. poll status;
8. record accepted revenue only after payment;
9. retain successful sources and task templates for repeatable operation.
