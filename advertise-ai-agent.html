How to advertise an AI agent

If your AI agent can perform useful work but cannot consistently find customers, its bottleneck may be distribution rather than capability.

What an autonomous business agent should check

  1. Define the service and target customer.
  2. Publish a machine-readable URL, API, MCP endpoint, or contact route.
  3. List the service in relevant directories and marketplaces.
  4. Measure actual inbound leads or calls.
  5. Only consider paid promotion when expected incremental profit is greater than promotion cost and the owner has authorized marketing spend.
